2. İşlenen bilgiler

  • Yerel oyun verileri: 8×8 oyun tahtası, skor ve rekor, devam eden oyun, yıldız ve güçlendirici envanteri, günlük görevler, ayarlar ve gizlilik seçimleri cihazda kaydedilebilir.

Hukuki dayanak ve rıza gereksinimi veri türüne ve bulunduğunuz bölgeye göre değişir.

Privacy Policy

1. Scope and contact

This policy explains data processing in the Android game CUBELORA (com.adamorrowgames.cubelora). Developer brand: adamorrow. Existing publisher contact: TERVENYX GAMES. Privacy and support contact: tinytailsuniverse01@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

  • Local game data: the 8×8 board, scores and best score, ongoing runs, stars and booster inventory, daily challenges, settings and privacy choices may be stored on your device.
  • Technical data: integrated services may process app version, device and operating-system information, IP address, diagnostics and pseudonymous service identifiers.
  • Optional analytics: when you grant analytics permission, Firebase Analytics may process gameplay/session events, device context and an app-instance identifier. Game analytics collection is designed to remain disabled without permission.
  • Ads and consent: if ad serving is enabled, Unity LevelPlay, Google AdMob and associated partners may process IP addresses, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions and anti-fraud diagnostics. Google UMP handles advertising privacy choices.
  • In-app purchases: Google Play and Unity IAP may process product IDs, transaction status and verification information for products such as Remove Ads. The game does not directly collect your payment card number.

3. Purposes of processing

Information may be processed to operate the game, preserve progress and settings, verify purchase entitlements, provide optional analytics, serve ads when permitted, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors. Legal bases and consent requirements depend on the data type and your location.

4. Third-party services

The app uses, or may use depending on release configuration, Unity Gaming Services (including Authentication and Remote Config), Unity LevelPlay and mediated ad networks, Google AdMob/Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), Firebase Analytics and Google Play Billing. Providers operate under their own policies: Unity and Google. Relevant mediated ad partners may also be described in the in-app consent interface.

5. Consent and choices

Analytics consent and advertising privacy choices are separate. You can change analytics permission in Settings. Advertising privacy options are offered in the app when required by applicable rules and Google UMP. Withdrawing consent may not immediately erase information previously lawfully processed by all providers.

6. Retention, deletion and requests

Local progress and settings may remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact tinytailsuniverse01@gmail.com. Some requests may require contacting the relevant provider separately.

7. Security and international transfers

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Third-party providers may process data outside your country subject to applicable legal safeguards and their terms. No electronic transmission or storage is completely risk-free.

8. Children's privacy

The game does not ask players to enter a name, email address or phone number to create an account. Applicable children's privacy and advertising rules must be reflected in the Google Play target-audience declaration and release settings. Parents or guardians with concerns can contact tinytailsuniverse01@gmail.com.

9. Your rights and updates

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, erasure, objection and complaint to a competent authority. We may revise this policy as game features, SDKs or legal obligations change; the revision date will be updated here.
